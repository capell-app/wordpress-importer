## What it does for you

WordPress Importer brings your WordPress posts, pages, and media references into Capell. It works with Migration Assistant: you point it at a WordPress export, preview the parsed content and preserved WordPress metadata, import, and verify everything came across.

## Your screens

- **WordPress import**: where you provide your WordPress export and start the import.
- **WordPress metadata preview**: where categories, tags, author, permalink, media, Gutenberg, and shortcode details are preserved for review.
- **Results**: what came across and anything that needs attention.

## What you can do

- Connect a WordPress export.
- Preserve old categories, tags, authors, permalinks, and media references as WordPress metadata.
- Import posts, pages, and media.
- Verify the imported content.

## Where to find it

It appears as a source inside **Migration Assistant**. Install Migration Assistant first, then start a WordPress import from there.

## Good to know

- Bring your **WordPress** posts into Capell without re-typing them.
- Review old categories and tags in the preview metadata before deciding how to organise imported content in Capell.
- Verify that images came across after the import.
